# AX_3_2

![AX_3_2_network](./AX_3_2_network.svg)

* * * * * * * * * *

## Introduction

`AX_3_2` combines `AX_OR_3` and `AX_SPLIT_2` into a single, complete module: Three `AX` sources (`IN1`, `IN2`, `IN3`) are ORed together to form a single signal and then distributed to two destinations (`OUT1`, `OUT2`).



## Function Blocks (FBs) Used

### Sub-Blocks: AX_3_2

- **Type**: SubAppType
- **Internal FBs Used**:

- **AX_OR_3**: `adapter::booleanOperators::AX_OR_3` — ORs `IN1`/`IN2`/`IN3`.

- **AX_SPLIT_2**: `adapter::events::unidirectional::AX_SPLIT_2` — splits the OR result to two outputs.

- **How it works**: `IN1`/`IN2`/`IN3` → `AX_OR_3.OUT` → `AX_SPLIT_2.IN` → `OUT1`/`OUT2`.

## Program Flow and Connections

1. `IN1` → `AX_OR_3.IN1`; `IN2` → `AX_OR_3.IN2`; `IN3` → `AX_OR_3.IN3`.

2. `AX_OR_3.OUT` → `AX_SPLIT_2.IN`.

3. `AX_SPLIT_2.OUT1` → `OUT1`; `AX_SPLIT_2.OUT2` → `OUT2`.

## Application Scenarios

- Merging three `AX` sources (e.g., three command paths) whose combined output must be distributed to two independent destinations.


## Comparison with Similar Building Blocks

For other source/destination counts: [`AX_2_3`](./AX_2_3.md) (2→3), [`AX_3_3`](./AX_3_3.md) (3→3).

## Summary

`AX_3_2` is a simple wiring aid that combines three `AX` sources using an OR operation and distributes them to two destinations—without its own state logic.
